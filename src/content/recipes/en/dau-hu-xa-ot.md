*Đậu hũ xả ớt* is golden outside and soft inside, with lemongrass and chili fried dry and fragrant and sprinkled over the tofu. With little water on its surface, the tofu browns more easily when fried (see [the Maillard reaction](/principles/maillard-reaction/)). The lemongrass is fried slowly over medium-low heat, so that its fragrance comes out (see [spices and aromatics](/principles/aromatics/)).

## Steps

1. Press the tofu dry and cut it into slices about 1.3 cm thick. If it holds a lot of water, weigh it down for a few minutes before cutting.
2. Toss the tofu gently with the lemongrass, shallot, chili and salt, and marinate for 15 minutes.
3. Heat a skillet over medium heat and add the oil. Scrape the aromatics off the tofu back into the bowl and keep them. Put the tofu in the pan in a single layer, fry until both sides are golden and slightly crisp, and take it out.
4. Pour off the oil and burnt bits in the pan. Turn to medium-low heat and add the aromatics used for marinating the tofu. Stir-fry until light golden, then add the garlic and a little salt, and keep stir-frying until golden and crisp. Sprinkle the aromatics over the tofu.

## Key points

- **Tofu sticks to the pan and won't brown**: the surface is too wet, so the water evaporates first and the tofu has trouble browning ([the Maillard reaction](/principles/maillard-reaction/)). Press it dry first and add it to the pan only once the oil is hot.
- **Tofu is fried tough and chewy**: it was fried too long and lost too much water ([protein coagulation](/principles/protein-coagulation/)). Take it out as soon as both sides are golden.
- **Lemongrass is coarse and tough, or bitter**: the outer fibres are too coarse, or the aromatics burnt ([spices and aromatics](/principles/aromatics/)). Use only the tender core, chop it fine, stir-fry over medium-low heat and add the garlic later.
- **For lunch boxes**: pack in a shallow container, refrigerate soon after it stops steaming, and reheat the next day until the centre reaches 74°C (165°F). The aromatics soften in the fridge.
