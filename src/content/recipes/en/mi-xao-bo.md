In *mì xào bò* the egg noodles are springy, the beef is tender, the vegetables keep some crunch, and a thin oyster sauce coats the noodles. The noodles are boiled until slightly underdone so they do not turn mushy when returned to the pan (see [starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/)). The beef is seared on its own in an uncrowded pan so it browns easily (see [the Maillard reaction](/principles/maillard-reaction/)).

## Steps

1. Toss the beef with the salt, black pepper, cornstarch and water, then mix in the oil, and marinate for 15–30 minutes. Mix the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, sugar and water into the sauce.
2. Cook the egg noodles according to the package, lifting them out 1 minute early. Rinse under cold water and drain well.
3. Heat the pan over medium heat, add a little oil and turn to medium-high. Spread the beef in one layer and leave it for 20–30 seconds, add half the garlic and stir a few more times, until most of the beef has changed colour but is still slightly pink. Remove it.
4. Add a little more oil to the pan and turn to medium heat. Stir-fry the carrot for 30 seconds, add the onion and stir-fry for about 45 seconds, then add the remaining garlic and stir-fry until fragrant.
5. Turn to medium-high or high heat, add the napa cabbage and bok choy, and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes, until the vegetables are soft but still crisp.
6. Add the noodles and toss for about 30 seconds, until they are hot through, then pour in the sauce and toss to combine. Return the beef and the juices on its plate, stir-fry quickly until the beef is cooked through and the vegetables are still crisp-tender, and plate with black pepper on top.

## Key points

- **Mushy noodles**: they were boiled too long and keep absorbing water after returning to the pan ([starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/)). Lift them out early by the package time, then rinse and drain.
- **Grey, tough beef**: the pan was too full, so the meat released water and the heat was not concentrated enough ([the Maillard reaction](/principles/maillard-reaction/)). Spread it out and cook in batches, and take it out while it is still slightly pink.
- **Vegetables not crisp**: they stayed in the pan too long. Cook the firmer carrot first, then add the cabbage; stir-fry quickly over high heat and add the noodles as soon as the vegetables soften ([heat transfer](/principles/heat-transfer/)).
