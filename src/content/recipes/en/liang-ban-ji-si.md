Cold shredded chicken has to be tender, and that depends on how the chicken is poached. Chicken breast has little connective tissue. If the water stays at a hard boil, the [muscle proteins contract](/principles/protein-coagulation/) and squeeze out moisture. Once the chicken is cooked, cool it quickly so the residual heat does not carry on and dry out the meat.

Shred the breast into fine strands along the grain. The larger surface area means a small amount of dressing coats it evenly. The [sourness](/principles/acid/) of the black vinegar makes the salty savouriness clearer, and the sugar softens the sourness. Sichuan pepper oil brings a numbing fragrance; if you use toasted sesame oil instead, the taste is of sesame, a different flavour.

## Steps

1. Thaw frozen chicken breast fully in the fridge first. Slice the ginger. If the two ends of the breast differ a lot in thickness, cut it across into two pieces of similar thickness, so the thin end doesn't dry out before the thick end is cooked.
2. Put the poaching water, ginger slices, chicken breast and Shaoxing wine in a pot and heat uncovered to a boil, skimming off the foam. Then turn to low heat so the surface bubbles only gently, with the chicken fully under water.
3. Poach for 5–7 minutes after the water boils. Insert a thermometer sideways into the centre of the thickest part and lift the chicken out at 74°C (165°F). If it hasn't reached that, keep poaching gently and measure again after a while.
4. Put the cooked breast in a clean container and rinse it with cooled boiled water to bring the temperature down, then lift out and drain. When it is no longer hot to the touch, shred it along the grain into fine strands of as even a thickness as you can, leaving no thick lumps.
5. In a bowl, put the Sichuan pepper oil, light soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar and half the salt, and stir until the sugar and salt have mostly dissolved. Toss a mouthful of chicken with a little of the dressing and taste, then decide whether to add the rest of the salt.
6. Pour the dressing over the chicken in portions, loosening the strands gently with chopsticks, and toss until everything is glossy. If you want cilantro, green onion or chili oil, mix them in last. Drain the chicken before tossing so water doesn't pool at the bottom of the bowl.
7. It can be eaten once tossed. To prepare ahead, put the cooked chicken in a shallow container in the fridge and dress it just before eating. Leave it at room temperature for no more than 2 hours in total, and eat refrigerated chicken within 2–3 days.

## Key points

- **The shredded chicken is dry and woody**: the water was at a hard boil, or the cooked chicken stayed in the hot water. The contraction described in [Protein Denaturation and Coagulation](/principles/protein-coagulation/) squeezes the moisture out. Poach gently instead, and lift it out for cooling as soon as the centre is cooked.
- **Cooked outside and still cold in the centre**: the thick breast was not thawed, so it heated unevenly (see [Heat Transfer](/principles/heat-transfer/)). Thaw it fully and measure the thickest part rather than relying on "a couple more minutes".
- **Bland dressing and a puddle in the bowl**: the chicken was not drained after cooling, and the water diluted the dressing. Remove the surface water first, then season in portions. When adding [acid](/principles/acid/) or salt, taste with a mouthful of chicken.
