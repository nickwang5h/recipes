*Polpettone* is the Italian "big meatball": the meat mixture is shaped into one loaf and baked, and sliced it is tender and savoury. This one is wrapped in smoked bacon, and the mixture also has mushrooms and mortadella.

The proteins in the meat and egg set with heat and bind the mixture into one piece (see [protein denaturation and coagulation](/principles/protein-coagulation/)). The mushrooms are cooked first to bring out their flavour, and the bacon browns in the oven, adding another layer of [Maillard reaction](/principles/maillard-reaction/) flavour.

## Steps

1. Chop the shallot and crush the garlic. Put olive oil and a small piece of butter in a pan and cook the shallot and garlic until soft. Add the mushrooms cut into small pieces, cook until done, season with salt and black pepper, and let cool.
2. Chop the mortadella and put it in a large bowl with the ground beef and pork, Parmigiano, bread crumbs, mushrooms and egg. Add salt, black pepper and nutmeg, and mix well.
3. Put the meat mixture on parchment paper, press it firm and shape it into a log. Cover the surface completely with the smoked bacon slices and place it in a rectangular baking pan lined with parchment.
4. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Cover the loaf with aluminum foil and bake for 20 minutes, then uncover and bake another 35–40 minutes, until the surface is browned and a thermometer inserted in the centre reads 71°C (160°F).
5. Take it out and rest for 10 minutes, then slice.

## Key points

- **The loaf falls apart**: the mixture was not mixed evenly, or not pressed firm when shaped. Mix until even, and press it down firmly by hand before it goes into the pan. See [protein denaturation and coagulation](/principles/protein-coagulation/).
- **It is dry when cut**: it baked too long, and the meat kept shrinking and losing water. Take it out when the centre reaches 71°C (160°F).
- **For lunch boxes**: refrigerate after slicing, and reheat the next day until the centre of each slice reaches 74°C (165°F).
