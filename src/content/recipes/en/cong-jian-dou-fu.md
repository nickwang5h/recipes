Pan-fried tofu with green onions is golden and lightly fragrant outside and still tender inside. The aroma comes from two ends. The tofu is first blotted dry and fried to a golden face on the bottom of the pan, where the [Maillard reaction](/principles/maillard-reaction/) gives the fried flavour. The chopped green onion is held back until just before the dish comes out of the pan, to keep its fresh aroma (see [Spices and Aromatics](/principles/aromatics/)).

Cut into thin slices, the tofu lies flat against the pan and heats evenly (see [Heat Transfer: Conduction, Convection and Radiation](/principles/heat-transfer/)). The tofu is fried first and set aside, the chili is cooked soft separately, and the two only need a short toss together at the end. Choose firm white tofu and drain it before it goes into the pan; soft, watery tofu does not hold its slices well.

## Steps

1. Drain the white tofu of its package liquid and blot off the surface water. Cut it into slices about 5 mm thick and lay them flat on a plate, not stacked in layers and without pressing hard.
2. Wash the green onion, trim the root and chop it. Wash the chili, remove the stem and seeds and cut it into small pieces about 1 cm square. Dissolve a little salt and the bouillon in 10 ml of water, for use when everything is combined.
3. Add 10 ml of oil to a frying pan and swirl to cover the bottom. Heat until the oil flows easily and the corners of the tofu sizzle softly when they touch it. Turn the heat to low and lay the tofu in a single layer; if the pan is small, fry in batches and do not stack.
4. When the bottom is golden and the surface has firmed up so the spatula can lift a whole slice gently, flip it and fry until the other side is golden too. If the colour is still pale and the tofu sticks to the pan, wait a little longer instead of scraping at it. Remove the tofu once both sides are fried.
5. If the pan lacks oil, add about 3 ml, then add the chili and stir-fry for about 3 minutes. Press the pieces lightly with the spatula so they stay in contact with the pan, and cook until the surface is slightly wrinkled and soft. If dark spots appear too early, lower the heat.
6. Return the tofu to the pan and turn it gently over medium heat for about 1 minute so the chili's aroma coats it. Pour in the dissolved seasoning water, raise the heat and cook off most of the water, leaving only a thin layer of sauce moistening the tofu; do not boil the tofu apart.
7. Taste a small piece of tofu and add salt if it is bland. Sprinkle in the chopped green onion and toss gently; as soon as the green onion aroma opens up, turn off the heat and plate.

## Key points

- **Tofu won't brown and water collects in the pan**: the surface is too wet or the pan is too crowded, so all the heat goes into evaporating water (see [the Maillard reaction](/principles/maillard-reaction/)). Blot off the surface water and fry in a single layer, in batches.
- **Tofu falls apart when flipped**: the tofu is too soft, cut unevenly, or the bottom has not yet formed a firm crust (see [heat transfer](/principles/heat-transfer/)). Choose firm tofu and wait until the spatula can lift a whole slice before flipping.
- **Green onion tastes bitter and has no fresh aroma**: the chopped green onion sat too long on the hot pan, so the aroma escaped and a scorched smell appeared (see [spices and aromatics](/principles/aromatics/)). Sprinkle it in just before the dish comes out, toss, and plate straight away.
