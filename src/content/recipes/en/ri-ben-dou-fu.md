Egg tofu is crisp outside and silky inside, but it is delicate and breaks easily when turned. The fix is to coat it thinly in starch before frying: the [starch gelatinizes](/principles/starch-gelatinization/) on the surface and keeps losing water, forming a crust that can hold the soft centre. The tofu goes back into the pan last, just long enough for the sauce to coat it, so both the light crispness outside and the smooth inside survive.

The savoury version is seasoned with light soy sauce and oyster sauce; for a sweet and sour version, add ketchup and sugar as a pair. Prepare the vegetables before you fry the tofu, so the crisp crust doesn't go soft while it waits. Cut the carrot thin so it cooks through with a short stir-fry; leave the bell pepper a little crisp, and the two need not be cut the same thickness.

## Steps

1. Prepare the vegetables. Cut the bell pepper into thin pieces, the carrot into thin slices and chop the garlic; if using ham sausage or onion, slice them. Soak the wood ear mushrooms through (about 20 minutes), remove the hard stem ends, then measure by the soaked weight.
2. Take the egg tofu out of its tube and cut into rounds about 1 cm thick. Pour off the liquid that has seeped onto the plate and move the pieces by supporting them from underneath, without pinching the soft centres with chopsticks.
3. Spread the starch on a flat plate and coat the tofu lightly on both sides and the edges. Shake off the loose starch; an even white surface is enough, with no thick drifts.
4. Fry the tofu in 150 ml of oil. Once the oil is hot, lay the pieces in gently, keeping bubbles around them all the time and the oil not smoking. Fry in batches with space between the pieces for 2–3 minutes, until the edges are golden and the bottom crust can lift in one piece, then flip. Fry about 2 minutes more after flipping, until both sides are golden, then take them out, drain the oil and spread them out.
5. Add 15 ml of oil to a wok and stir-fry the garlic and the optional onion until fragrant. Add the carrot, bell pepper and any other optional vegetables and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes, until the carrot is soft and the wood ear cooked through, with no liquid pooling on the bottom.
6. Add the light soy sauce, oyster sauce and any other seasoning you are using. Mix first and taste, then add salt a little at a time; leave even more room if using bouillon powder. For the sweet and sour version, add the sugar and ketchup at this step and stir-fry until the sugar dissolves.
7. Return the tofu gently to the pan and push it around by lifting from underneath. When the colour is even and the sauce has coated it, plate immediately and don't toss it back and forth any more.

## Key points

- **The tofu breaks as soon as it is turned**: the crust hasn't set, or the coating was too thick and the inside still wet and sticky. Fry in batches in the manner of [oil temperature and deep frying](/principles/oil-temperature/), and flip only when the underside lifts off the pan in one piece.
- **The crisp crust goes soft quickly**: the vegetables were wet, or the tofu was cooked too long after returning to the pan. Stir-fry the vegetables dry first and toss only briefly at the end; once the [starch crust](/principles/starch-gelatinization/) absorbs water it softens again.
- **The sauce is too salty**: oyster sauce, light soy sauce and bouillon powder all carry salt. Count them all in your [seasoning with salt](/principles/salt/); the amount of salt is only an upper limit, so don't add it all at once.
