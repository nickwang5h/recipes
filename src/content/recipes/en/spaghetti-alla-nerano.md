There is no cream in the sauce of spaghetti alla Nerano, yet it is silky enough to coat every strand, and all of the sweetness comes from the fried zucchini. The dish comes from Nerano, a small fishing village on the Sorrento peninsula.

Two things make it work. The zucchini is fried until its edges are golden, which turns the flavour from bland to sweet (see [oil temperature and deep frying](/principles/oil-temperature/)). Then the starch in the pasta water, the butter and the fat of the cheese turn the zucchini into a creamy sauce (see [emulsification](/principles/emulsification/) and [starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/)).

## Steps

1. Wash the zucchini and cut it into rounds 3–4 mm thick. Grate the cheese and tear the basil by hand.
2. Pour the olive oil into a frying pan and heat over medium heat. Fry the zucchini in 3–4 batches, 5–6 minutes per batch, until golden on both sides, and lift onto paper towels to drain.
3. Bring a large pot of water to a boil, add salt and cook the pasta for the time on the package, until it has some bite. Before draining, set aside a cup of the pasta water.
4. Leave a little frying oil in the pan, add the garlic and fry until golden, then remove it. Add the zucchini and stir-fry for 1 minute, then add the drained pasta.
5. Turn off the heat. Add the cheese, butter and basil, and toss while adding a little pasta water, until the cheese has melted and the sauce is thick and silky. Sprinkle on black pepper and serve right away.

## Key points

- **The zucchini is bitter**: the oil was too hot and the edges burned. Keep the heat at medium, do not overload each batch, keep the oil from dropping too low, and do not let it smoke. See [oil temperature and deep frying](/principles/oil-temperature/).
- **The zucchini is limp and has no golden edge**: too much went in at once, the oil temperature dropped, and the zucchini gave off water. Fry in small batches.
- **The cheese clumps and the sauce splits into oil**: the pan is too hot. Turn off the heat before adding the cheese; if the sauce is too dry, add a little pasta water and toss hard. See [emulsification](/principles/emulsification/).
- **The pasta dries out after sitting for a while**: it is still absorbing the sauce. Eat it as soon as it is made.
