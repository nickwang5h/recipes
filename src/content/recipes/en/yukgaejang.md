*Yukgaejang* is a spicy soup of shredded beef, fernbrake, bean sprouts and a lot of green onion. The green onion turns sweet once it is soft, the fernbrake is chewy, and each spoonful has both shreds of meat and vegetables. The brisket is first simmered whole for the broth, and once the [collagen](/principles/collagen-gelatin/) has softened and the fibres come apart, it is torn into thick shreds and returned to the pot.

The red oil on the soup comes from toasted sesame oil and Korean chili flakes. When the powder meets warm fat, colour and aroma spread quickly (see [spices and aromatics](/principles/aromatics/)); mixing in the moist meat and vegetables straight away keeps it from scorching dry. The beef broth, shiitake and soup soy sauce together supply [umami](/principles/umami/), so there is still a meaty base under the heat.

## Steps

1. Put the brisket pieces, about 8 cm long, in a pot with the onion, the rinsed dried shiitake and 3000 ml of water. Heat over medium-high heat for about 10 minutes, until the surface boils, and skim off the foam.
2. Turn to medium heat and simmer for 1 hour with the lid left ajar, keeping a steady bubble, until a fork can separate the thickest piece along the grain. If it will not shred, cook 10 minutes more, until the meat is tender.
3. Lift out the beef and shiitake and let them sit on a plate for 10 minutes, until you can handle them; discard the onion. Strain the broth and skim off the extra fat. Measure out 1680–1920 ml, topping up from the reserved hot water if short, and set the leftover broth aside.
4. Tear the beef along the grain into thick shreds about 6 cm long, and slice the shiitake. Rinse and drain the cooked fernbrake, remove the tough stems and cut into pieces about 6 cm long. Split the white parts of the green onions lengthwise, then cut them into pieces together with the green parts.
5. In a separate pot, heat 1500 ml of water for about 8 minutes, until it boils steadily. Blanch the bean sprouts for 1 minute, until the stems are slightly soft but still stand up, then lift out, rinse in cold water and drain.
6. Add the toasted sesame oil to a small pot and heat on the lowest heat for 30 seconds, until the oil flows easily. Add the chili flakes and stir for about 15 seconds, until the oil turns red and the powder is a moist paste, then turn off the heat at once. Mix in the beef, fernbrake, shiitake, minced garlic, 1 tbsp of salt and 0.5 tbsp of the soup soy sauce.
7. Pour the chili, meat and vegetable mixture back into the broth, add the remaining 0.5 tbsp of soup soy sauce and heat over medium-high heat for about 5 minutes, until it returns to a boil. Turn to medium heat, cover and cook for 10 minutes, until the fernbrake and shreds of meat are coated in the red soup.
8. Add the bean sprouts and green onions and cook over medium heat for 10 minutes, until the white parts of the green onion are soft and the sprouts no longer taste raw, while the fernbrake can still be picked up. Finally taste the soup and add salt and black pepper, and ladle into deep bowls to serve with white rice.

## Key points

- **The shreds are tough and won't tear apart however you pull**: the connective tissue in the brisket has not softened enough. Cook it in the broth until a fork can separate it, and only then season (see [collagen and gelatin](/principles/collagen-gelatin/)).
- **The soup is red but tastes burnt and bitter**: the chili powder stayed in hot oil too long. Use the lowest heat, and turn it off and mix in the wet ingredients as soon as the oil turns red; chili oil that has turned black must be remade (see [spices and aromatics](/principles/aromatics/)).
- **The soup is only hot, with an empty base**: the beef broth is too diluted by water. After straining, measure out the stated volume before combining it with the meat and vegetables, and set the saltiness only at the end (see [umami](/principles/umami/)).
