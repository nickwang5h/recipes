*Cơm gà Hội An* puts turmeric rice cooked in chicken broth and a sour, fragrant shredded chicken on the same plate. The chicken is torn into shreds and tossed with thin onion, lime juice and Vietnamese coriander, and the rice takes up the broth from cooking the chicken, so every grain tastes of chicken. Vietnamese coriander, *rau răm*, has a bright, slightly peppery aroma and is an important flavour in this Hội An home dish.

Taking out the breast and the legs separately keeps the breast from turning dry while you wait for the legs to cook through (see [heat transfer](/principles/heat-transfer/)). The rice grains are coated in oil first, then cook in the chicken broth through [starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/), and the [acidity](/principles/acid/) of the shredded chicken goes with the fatty flavour of the rice. Drain the onion, and toss in the herbs last and lightly, for a loose, fresh result.

## Steps

1. Divide the chicken into legs, wings, boneless breast and carcass. Put the legs, wings and carcass in a pot with the ginger, green onion, the salt for the broth and 1680 ml of water. Cook over medium-high heat for about 10 minutes, until the surface boils, skim off all the foam, then turn to low heat.
2. Add the breast, keeping it submerged, and cook for about 2 minutes, until the broth returns to a gentle simmer, then turn off the heat, cover and let it cook in the residual heat. Start checking the breast at 25 minutes; the legs need about 40 minutes. Test the thickest part of each separately, and take them out only when they reach 74°C (165°F). If not, return to low heat and keep cooking, testing every 5 minutes.
3. Soak the cooked chicken pieces in the cold water and ice for 8 minutes, until the skin and meat no longer feel hot. Drain, pull the meat from the bones, cover and refrigerate. Put the bones back into the broth and simmer over low heat for 45 minutes, until the broth has a clear chicken flavour, then strain. Measure out 600 ml for cooking the rice and keep the rest as a side broth.
4. Rinse the rice and drain it for 10 minutes, until nothing drips from the sieve. Put 4 tbsp of oil in a heavy-bottomed pot and heat over medium heat for about 1 minute, until it flows smoothly. Add the rice and turmeric and stir for 3 minutes, until each grain is evenly coated in oil and pale yellow.
5. Pour in 600 ml of hot broth and cook over medium heat for 2–3 minutes, until small holes appear on the surface and the water level drops to the rice. Turn to the lowest heat, cover and cook for 10 minutes, then turn off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes, until the centre of the grains is soft through. Fluff the rice, cover again and leave for 5 minutes so the remaining surface moisture disperses evenly.
6. Put the onion in 250 ml of ice-cold water with 40 ml of lime juice and 1.5 tsp sugar, and soak for 15 minutes, until the sharpness is milder but the thin slices are still crisp. Drain well. Tear the chicken into fine strips along the grain.
7. Mix the rest of the lime juice, the sugar, the pepper and half of the salt for tossing the chicken, add the shredded chicken and the onion, toss lightly, and rest for 5 minutes, until the chicken is evenly sour and salty; taste and add salt. Mix the Vietnamese coriander into the rice just before plating. Put rice and shredded chicken on each of four plates, and serve the chili on the side.
8. Heat the remaining broth over low heat for about 3–5 minutes, until it comes back to a simmer, and serve in small bowls with the chicken rice.

## Key points

- **Dry, stringy breast**: it was cooked as long as the legs, so the proteins in the outer layer kept shrinking. Check each part separately and take the breast out first once it is done (see [heat transfer](/principles/heat-transfer/)).
- **Rice that is undercooked or scorched on the bottom**: the broth was not hot enough, or the heat stayed high after the lid went on. Add hot broth, turn to the lowest heat once the water has dropped to the rice, and rest it for the full time (see [starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/)).
- **The shredded chicken turns wet and limp when tossed**: the onion carried its soaking water and diluted the lime juice. Drain the onion well, let the seasoned chicken rest only briefly, and add the Vietnamese coriander just before plating (see [acid](/principles/acid/)).
