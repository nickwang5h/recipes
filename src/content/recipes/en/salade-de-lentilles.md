French lentil salad has soft, whole lentils, a tangy mustard vinaigrette, and the fresh taste of shallot and parsley. The lentils are tossed with the vinaigrette while still warm, and the salad is also good once cooled.

Dijon mustard helps the oil and vinegar blend into a smooth vinaigrette (see [Emulsification](/principles/emulsification/)), and the [acid](/principles/acid/) of the vinegar makes the lentils taste fresher. Lentils are mild, so they depend entirely on the vinaigrette, shallot and parsley for flavour. Toss in the vinaigrette while the lentils are still warm, so they can absorb it.

## Steps

1. Rinse the lentils and put them in a pot with cold water, the onion studded with the clove, the bay leaf and the thyme. Bring to a boil, then turn to low and keep the surface barely bubbling.
2. After 10 minutes, add the diced carrot and keep cooking until the lentils are soft all the way through but still whole, about 20–25 minutes in total. Drain, and remove the onion, clove and herbs.
3. While the lentils cook, make the vinaigrette: whisk together the mustard, red wine vinegar, salt and black pepper, then pour in the olive oil while whisking, and finally stir in the minced shallot.
4. While the lentils are still warm, pour on the vinaigrette, add the diced celery and toss gently. Taste, and add salt, vinegar and black pepper as needed.
5. Sprinkle with the parsley and serve warm, or pack into containers promptly and chill, then eat cold.

## Key points

- **Lentils turn to mush**: the water boiled too hard or they cooked too long, and the skins tend to split. Keep the heat low and drain as soon as the lentils are soft but still whole.
- **Flavour is uneven**: the vinaigrette separates if it stands. Whisk it again before tossing (see [Emulsification](/principles/emulsification/)). Tossing while the lentils are warm helps them take up the seasoning.
- **For lunch boxes**: pack in a shallow container and chill promptly, and eat within 2–3 days of chilling. Leave it at room temperature for about 15 minutes before eating, and keep the total time at room temperature under 2 hours.
