Chirashi sushi is the sushi Japanese families make for celebrations: a big platter of sushi rice mixed with sweet-savoury shiitake, burdock and kanpyo, covered with shredded egg crepe and dotted with shrimp and snow peas, brought to the table to share.

The rice is made the same way as [sushi rice](/recipes/sushi-meshi/): the vinegar goes in while the rice is hot, the [gelatinized](/principles/starch-gelatinization/) grains soak it up, and the sweet-sour taste becomes the base of the rice (see [acid](/principles/acid/)). Every mix-in and topping must be completely cool, and the mix-in squeezed of its liquid before it goes in; warm or wet ingredients make the rice soggy.

## Steps

1. Soak the dried shiitake in the 120 ml water until fully soft.
2. Squeeze out the shiitake, strain the soaking water to remove grit and keep it; cut off the stems and slice the caps thinly.
3. Scrape the brown skin off the burdock with the back of a knife, then shave it into thin slivers, turning it as if sharpening a pencil. Keep the slivers in water so they don't brown, changing the water once or twice.
4. Rinse the kanpyo quickly, rub it with the salt and rinse it clean.
5. Boil the kanpyo for 3 minutes, transfer to iced water, squeeze dry and cut into short pieces.
6. Cut the carrot into fine julienne.
7. Put the shiitake, drained burdock and kanpyo in a medium saucepan, add the dashi, shiitake soaking water, sake, mirin, 1 tbsp sugar and soy sauce, bring to a simmer over medium heat and skim off the foam.
8. Turn the heat to low, set a drop lid (a small lid resting right on the food) on top and simmer; when the liquid is nearly gone, add the carrot and cook until almost no liquid is left, about 20–25 minutes in all. Spread it on a plate and let it cool completely.
9. Make the sushi rice as in the sushi rice recipe, using the rice, water, kombu and sushi vinegar listed here, and fan it until it is about body temperature.
10. Whisk the eggs, mirin, salt and starch slurry together and strain through a fine sieve.
11. Heat a nonstick pan over medium heat, wipe it with a paper towel dipped in the oil, turn to medium-low, pour in just enough egg to cover the bottom and tilt the pan to spread it. Cover and cook about 1 minute, until the surface is no longer wet. Take it out to cool and cook the rest the same way.
12. Stack the crepes, roll them up tightly, slice into fine shreds and loosen them with your fingers.
13. Pull the strings off the snow peas, blanch them for 1 minute in boiling water with a little salt, and cut them on the diagonal.
14. Devein the shrimp in their shells. Bring water to a boil, add a little salt and the sake, and cook the shrimp for about 2–3 minutes, until pink and 74°C (165°F) in the centre; lift out and peel.
15. Squeeze the liquid out of the mix-in and fold it into the sushi rice with a slicing motion of the paddle.
16. Cover the rice with the egg shreds, arrange the shrimp and snow peas on top, sprinkle with sesame and pile the shredded nori in the centre.

## Key points

- **The rice is soggy and clumped**: the mix-in wasn't squeezed dry, or something went in while still warm (see [starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/)). Let everything cool completely and squeeze the liquid out of the mix-in first.
- **The sweet-sour taste sits only on the surface**: the rice was seasoned after it had cooled, so the grains couldn't take it in (see [acid](/principles/acid/)). Have the sushi vinegar ready and season the rice the moment it is done.
- **The egg shreds are browned**: the pan was too hot. Turn to medium-low before pouring in the egg; if the pan is too hot, lift it off the heat for a few seconds.
- **Leftovers**: eat chirashi sushi within 2 hours at room temperature. Refrigerate what is left and eat it within 2–3 days.
