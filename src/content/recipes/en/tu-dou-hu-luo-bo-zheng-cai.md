The potato and carrot shreds in this steamed dish are wrapped in a soft, tender layer of flour and taste of green onion and garlic. The shreds are coated thinly in flour, steamed until cooked, and then stir-fried. The flour absorbs the water on the surface of the shreds, and when heated its [starch gelatinizes](/principles/starch-gelatinization/) into a soft coating. The flour has to be spread over every shred; with too much water it becomes a batter and the whole thing steams into one sticky sheet.

Steaming does the cooking, and the final quick stir-fry carries the green onion and garlic fragrance onto the coated shreds (see [spices and aromatics](/principles/aromatics/)). The shreds must be loosened as they steam, and you cannot tip a tight clump straight into the pot. Seasoning is only salt and MSG, and the green onion and garlic oil provide the savoury base.

## Steps

1. Cut the potatoes and carrots into fine shreds of even thickness. If the potato shreds were rinsed, drain them thoroughly and blot off the surface water; do not put wet carrot shreds into the bowl either. Prepare the chopped green onion, garlic pieces and dried chili pieces.
2. Sprinkle in the flour in portions and toss lightly, pulling apart any shreds that stick together. Stop when the surface has a thin coating and the shreds fall loose from a dry hand. Do not force the leftover flour in, and do not knead it into a clump.
3. Bring water to a boil in the steamer first. Spread the shreds loosely on a steamer tray lined with cloth, so steam can pass around them, and cover. Once steam rises, steam over high heat for about 5 minutes. Taste the potato shreds at the thickest spot; take them out only when there is no raw firmness and the coating has no raw flour taste.
4. Spread the steamed shreds out and loosen them gently with chopsticks while they are still warm. If the centre is not cooked, return them to the steamer for a bit longer; do not count on the final stir-fry to fix it.
5. Add the oil to the wok and heat until it flows easily. Add the green onion, garlic pieces and dried chili and stir-fry over medium heat until fragrant, about 40 seconds. While the garlic is still pale, add the steamed shreds, turn to medium-high heat and toss from the bottom to loosen them, about 20 seconds.
6. Add the salt and MSG and toss until the seasoning is even and the shreds are hot through, then take them out. Put in half the salt first, taste, and add more if needed.

## Key points

- **Steamed into a sticky clump**: too much water on the surface, so the flour forms a continuous paste (see [starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/)). Remove surface water before coating, sprinkle the flour on in portions, and steam only when a handful falls apart.
- **Hard in the middle, cooked outside**: spread too thick or cut uneven, so steam cannot carry heat evenly (see [heat transfer](/principles/heat-transfer/)). Spread loosely, go by the thickest spot having no raw firmness, and steam longer if needed.
- **Green onion and garlic taste bitter**: the aromatics were heated in the oil too long (see [aroma extraction](/principles/aromatics/)). Add the steamed shreds as soon as you smell the fragrance; if the small garlic pieces start to turn dark brown, lower the heat first.
