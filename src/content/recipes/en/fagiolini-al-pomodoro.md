Green beans cooked until soft and full of tomato juice are more like a stew than crisp stir-fried green beans, and they are good hot or cooled. This is a common home-style side dish in southern Italy.

The method has two tracks: the beans are first boiled soft in salted water (see [Blanching and Removing Off-Odours](/principles/blanching/)), while the sourness of the tomato keeps the sauce fresh (see [Acid: Balance, Softening and Stability](/principles/acid/)). Finally the two are combined in one pan and braised for a few minutes, so the beans absorb the sauce.

## Steps

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil, then add salt. Add the green beans and cook for about 15 minutes, until just slightly underdone, then lift out and drain.
2. While the beans cook, put the olive oil and garlic in a skillet and heat over medium heat until the garlic begins to bubble.
3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, add salt and chili flakes to taste, and cook over low heat for a few minutes.
4. Add the drained green beans to the pan, toss, cover and braise over low heat for about 5 minutes, until the beans are cooked through and the sauce coats them.
5. Before turning off the heat, taste the sauce and add a small pinch of salt if it is bland. Serve hot or cooled.

## Key points

- **The sauce is thin and the beans sit in liquid**: the beans were not drained well enough, or the tomato sauce was not reduced. Drain the beans well after cooking, and reduce the sauce to a single layer while braising.
- **The beans are hard and astringent**: they were undercooked. Boil until just slightly underdone first, then braise in the sauce until a fork can press through; if the beans are mature, cook them a little longer. Raw beans must not be eaten undercooked, so do not take them out early just to keep them bright green.
- **The beans turn grey**: braised for a long time with acidic tomato, the chlorophyll changes colour; this is normal and does not affect the flavour. See [Acid: Balance, Softening and Stability](/principles/acid/).
- **Lunch box**: refrigerate promptly after packing and keep sealed for up to 2 days. Take it out of the refrigerator and leave it at room temperature for about 30 minutes before eating.
