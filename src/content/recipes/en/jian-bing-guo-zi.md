Jianbing is a breakfast that wraps a thin crepe around egg, sauces and a youtiao, with a soft, chewy crepe and a crisp fried dough. Mung bean flour gives a beany fragrance, and wheat flour holds the crepe together as one whole sheet. The batter absorbs water and sets on the hot pan through [starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/). The egg spread over the crepe [coagulates](/principles/protein-coagulation/) with the heat and adds another layer under the soft crepe.

The filling is a [youtiao (Chinese crullers)](/recipes/you-tiao/), brushed with sweet bean sauce and fermented tofu. On the street the crepe is spread on a griddle with a bamboo spreader; at home, a 28 cm nonstick skillet works, tilting the pan to spread the thin batter. The key is a crepe that is thin and whole, a thin brush of sauce, and the youtiao added last so it is still crisp when you eat it.

## Steps

1. Put the mung bean flour and flour in a bowl. Add 100 ml of the water and mix into a smooth paste, then add the remaining 200 ml. Stir until no dry lumps remain, cover and rest for 10 minutes, until the flour has absorbed the water and the batter runs off a spoon in a continuous stream. Stir the settled flour back in before cooking.
2. Chop the green onion finely, mash the fermented tofu into a paste, and set the sauces and youtiao beside the stove. If the youtiao have gone cold, bake them in an oven preheated to 180°C (350°F) for 5 minutes, until the shell is crisp again and the inside is hot when you break one open. Leave freshly fried ones on a rack to let the steam escape.
3. Wipe a thin layer of oil in the nonstick pan and preheat over medium-low heat for about 1 minute, until poured batter spreads slowly. Ladle in a quarter of the batter and tilt the pan at once to cover the base. Cook for about 1 minute, until there is no wet batter on the surface and the edges begin to lift from the pan.
4. Crack in 1 egg, gently break the yolk with the back of a spoon and spread the egg out, then sprinkle on 1 tsp of green onion. Cook another 1–2 minutes, until the egg begins to set and the crepe can be lifted whole with a spatula. Do not flip while the crepe is still soft and sagging.
5. Loosen the edge with the spatula and flip the crepe so the egg side faces down. Cook about 1 minute more, until the egg is fully set with no clear white. The egg layer is cooked at 74°C (165°F); if it has not reached that, cook 30 seconds more.
6. Turn the heat to the lowest setting. On the upward-facing side, brush on 1 tsp of sweet bean sauce and 0.5 tsp of fermented tofu paste, plus 0.5 tsp of chili oil if you want it. Lay a youtiao on top, fold the crepe over it from both sides, then fold up the top and bottom ends and take it out at once.
7. Wipe the remaining thin film of oil evenly over the pan and repeat for the other 3 crepes, stirring the batter each time. Serve each one as soon as it is wrapped. Do not stack them to steam, and do not let the youtiao sit in the sauce beforehand.

## Key points

- **The batter clumps as soon as it hits the pan and will not spread**: the pan is too hot, and the batter sets on contact (see [heat transfer](/principles/heat-transfer/)). Take the pan off the heat for 1 minute to cool, then make the next one over medium-low heat.
- **It tears when flipped and sticks to the spatula**: [starch gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/) and egg coagulation are not finished. Wait until the crepe can be lifted whole before flipping. At home you can slide it onto a large pot lid and invert it back into the pan.
- **The egg layer is still wet and slippery**: the [protein](/principles/protein-coagulation/) has not had enough heat. Cook the egg side against the pan until set, and spread the egg white thin as you lay it down so it does not pool into a thick layer in the middle.
- **The youtiao is soft and the crepe is damp and sticky**: the sauce was brushed on too thickly, or the wrapped crepes were stacked and the steam wet the youtiao again (see [heat transfer](/principles/heat-transfer/)). Keep the sauce thin and close the wrap only when you are about to eat.
