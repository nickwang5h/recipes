Rice salad is fresh and savoury, with tomato, tuna and black olives between the grains of rice. After chilling, the starch in cooked rice retrogrades and firms up (see [Starch Gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/)), so taste the rice as it cooks, to get grains that are cooked through and still a little chewy.

The tuna, anchovies and black olives each bring saltiness, and the cherry tomatoes add sweetness, sourness and juice, which the cooled rice will absorb. So the salt goes in last: toss, taste, then add more (see [Salt: Seasoning, Osmosis and Curing](/principles/salt/)).

## Steps

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add salt. Add the rice and cook until cooked through but still a little firm to the bite. Drain and spread in a single layer on a shallow tray so the grains do not stick into a mass; once it stops steaming, put it in the refrigerator to cool.
2. Wash the cherry tomatoes and wipe off the surface water, so it does not dilute the seasoning later; halve them and put them in a large bowl with the drained, flaked tuna. Pit the black olives and chop the anchovies, and add both.
3. Add the fully cooled rice, drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with salt and black pepper, and toss gently so the cherry tomatoes and rice grains are not crushed. Finally scatter on the chopped parsley and refrigerate until serving.

## Key points

- **Rice grains clump**: the rice was cooked too soft and piled too thick as it cooled. Drain it when it still has some bite, spread it out to cool, and loosen it gently when mixing, without pressing hard or piling it into a lump (see [Starch Gelatinization](/principles/starch-gelatinization/)).
- **Too salty**: the anchovies and olives are both salty. Toss everything together and taste before adjusting with [salt](/principles/salt/).
- **For lunch boxes**: pack it and refrigerate, and keep it for 2 days at most; let it sit at room temperature for 15 minutes before eating so the rice softens slightly. Carry it in an insulated bag to keep it cold.
