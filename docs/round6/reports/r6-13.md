- **年糕汤**：牛肉清汤煮软薄片年糕，配蛋丝、葱和海苔。来源：[Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/tteokguk)、[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/tteokguk-korean-rice-cake-soup/)。
- **豆芽汤**：黄豆芽在鳀鱼清汤中短煮，豆头熟透、芽茎保留脆感。来源：[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/kongnamul-guk-soybean-sprout-soup/)、[Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/kongnamulguk)。
- **鸡粥**：香油炒米后用鸡汤煮软，后段加入蔬菜和鸡丝。来源：[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/dakjuk-korean-chicken-porridge/)。
- **紫菜包饭**：香油米饭包入牛肉、蛋皮、菠菜、胡萝卜和腌萝卜。来源：[Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/gimbap)、[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/gimbapkimbap-korean-dried-seaweed-rolls/)。
- **韩式炸酱面**：春酱炒香后与猪肉、蔬菜煮成酱，勾芡浇粗麦面。来源：[Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/jjajangmyeon)、[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/jajangmyeon-noodles-in-black-bean-sauce/)。
