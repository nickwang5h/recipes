- **辣炒鸡排**：鸡腿、包菜、红薯和年糕同锅炒，写清补水与收汁判断。来源：[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/dak-galbi/)、[Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/dakgalbi)。
- **辣炖鸡块**：鸡肉先煮、蔬菜后下，保留可拌饭的浓汤。来源：[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/dak-doritang-korean-spicy-chicken-stew/)。
- **韩式炸鸡**：纯淀粉薄衣、两次油炸，最后裹蒜香甜辣酱。来源：[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/yangnyeom-chicken-korean-fried-chicken/)、[Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/yangnyeom-tongdak)。
- **菜包白切肉**：五花肉煮软切片，配生菜、泡菜和盐渍小虾。来源：[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/bossam-boiled-pork-wraps/)、[Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/bo-ssam)。
- **韩式酱牛肉**：先煮软再酱卤，撕成细条作为配饭小菜。来源：[Korean Bapsang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/jangjorim-soy-braised-beef/)、[Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/jangjorim)。
